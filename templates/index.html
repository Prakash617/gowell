Welcome to Gowell Education Center

We are delighted to have you visit GOWELL EDUCATION CENTER. Students who wish to improve their language proficiency in order to communicate with Japanese speakers on certain topics are the target audience for the language program.

There are many cultural, religious, and even sentimental parallels between Nepal and Japan. The underground steam of history that penetrates both of our civilizations ties Nepal's and Japan's traditional traditions together.

The good connections that exist between Nepal and Japan are something that we have always and forever earned. Both of these nations have openly and diplomatically shared or traded varied cultural identities. Nepal has arranged a number of seminars and fellowship programs in an effort to recognize its citizens' concerns about the strengthened ties between the two nations. Seminars on language, culture, technology, and many other topics are held by numerous organizations and schools with the goal of raising young pupils' awareness and understanding.

People-to-people interchange of cultural norms and values is the foundation of the two nations' connection. Language and cultural instruction may offer a bridge model that is crucial in helping the young, active citizens of this nation build a deeper awareness of Japan and its advanced technologies.
